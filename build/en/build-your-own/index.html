How it works

You have done harder things than this.

Assisted self-serve means you build the bowl and we handle the fire. Here is the whole process, start to finish.

01

Take a bowl

Walk the fridge. Over 100 ingredients, fresh and frozen. Take what you like and skip what you don't.

02

Still wondering

How much will it cost me?

You pay by weight, at one price per 100g, so the bowl decides the bill. Load it with greens and noodles and it stays cheap. Load it with prawns and beef and it costs more. You can see the scale while it weighs.

How spicy is spicy?

Our Signature Spicy Malatang and Stir-Fry Spicy Pot are properly numbing, the way Sichuan intends. If that is not your thing, Pork Bone, Tomato and Golden Pickled carry no chilli heat at all.

Is there anything for vegetarians?

Plenty of the fridge is vegetarian, and the Umami Wild Mushroom Fried Rice is built for it. Note that most of our broths are started from pork or beef bones, so ask staff before choosing a base.

Is David's Master Pot halal?

No. We are not halal-certified in Australia, and our signature broths use a pork bone base. We would rather tell you plainly than have you find out at the table.

Do I need to book?

No. Walk in, take a bowl, join the queue at the scale. Most of our shops are built for turnover, not reservations.
