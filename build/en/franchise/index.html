Why this model works

01

No chefs to hire

Guests build their own bowls. Your kitchen simmers broth and runs a scale, which makes staffing dramatically simpler than a full à la carte kitchen.

02

Waste stays low

Self-serve by weight means you prep to demand, not to a fixed menu. Ingredients that don't sell today are still ingredients tomorrow.

03

Small footprint, fast turnover

Our shops are built for volume in tight retail sites. Food courts, high streets and shopping centres all work.

04

A brand people already queue for

You are not explaining a new concept to the market. Malatang already has an audience in Australia, and they already know the name.

How we got here

2017

September — David's Master Pot is founded and lands in Melbourne.

2018

17 November — the first shop opens on Melbourne's busiest street. The next day, we expand into Brisbane.

2019

A second Melbourne branch opens in March. The Chef David flagship follows in November.

2020

Five shops in Melbourne. David's Hot Pot reaches Docklands and Point Cook, and the group covers the Gold Coast and Adelaide by year end.

2023

Franchise shops span Victoria, South Australia and Queensland, taking the group to eight in Australia. Our signature hot pot beef reaches central Brisbane.

2024

Two new locations bring authentic malatang to more of the country.

2025

Three more shops. One mission: unmistakable hot pot, everywhere in Australia.

2026

Collingwood opens in August. Harbour Town on the Gold Coast follows in September.
