Small bites

Crispy Prawn Spring Rolls

Light, golden, and shatteringly crisp pastry sheets wrapped around a juicy, seasoned prawn filling. The perfect crunchy bite to start your meal.

Fried Crispy Pork

Premium pork neck pieces lightly battered and fried to a perfect golden crunch, then tossed in a subtle, aromatic spice seasoning. A wonderful contrast of crispy outside and tender inside.

Golden Red Bean Sesame Doughnut

Golden, crispy pastry puffs coated in toasted sesame seeds, opening up to a warm, sweet, and smooth red bean paste centre.

Crispy Mochi with Sticky Brown Sugar Glaze

Crispy on the outside with a wonderfully chewy, mochi-like centre, generously drizzled with a rich, sticky brown sugar syrup.

Pork and Prawn Wonton Soup

Delicate, handmade wontons packed with juicy pork and prawns, served in a clean, comforting, and clear seasoned broth. Pure comfort in a bowl.

Pork and Prawn Wonton in Chilli Oil

The same plump pork and prawn wontons, but served floating in a fragrant, fiery house-made chilli oil broth. Slippery, spicy, and incredibly satisfying.

Rice

Fragrant Shallot Oil & Egg Fried Rice

Fluffy, aromatic jasmine rice wok-fried with eggs and infused with our fragrant, house-made shallot oil. Simple, comforting, and the perfect sidekick to any spicy dish.

Umami Wild Mushroom Fried Rice

A premium, earthy fried rice tossed with a rich, savoury paste made from Maitake mushrooms. Deeply savoury, vegetarian-friendly, and packed with umami.

Steamed Jasmine Rice

A simple bowl of perfectly steamed, fragrant jasmine rice to help balance out the bold spices of your meal.
