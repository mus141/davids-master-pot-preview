The broth is ours. The bowl is yours.

We don't plate it. You do.

Most restaurants decide what you eat. We decided not to. Walk the fridge, take what you want, as much or as little, then pick the broth it swims in. Our job is the broth, simmered from scratch every morning. The rest of the bowl is yours.

Since 2018 that has taken us from one shop on Elizabeth Street to twenty across four states, which we take as a sign the country agrees.
